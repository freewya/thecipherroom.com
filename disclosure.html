What is an Affiliate Link?

NeonVault participates in affiliate marketing programs with select retailers and product vendors. When you click a product link marked with "View Deal" or "Explore Product" and complete a purchase, we may earn a commission from the retailer at no additional cost to you.

How Does This Affect You?

Zero additional cost: You pay the same price whether you use our link or go directly to the retailer.

Same products, same prices: Product availability, pricing, and shipping are determined entirely by the retailer.

Our Commitment to Honesty

  • Independence: Affiliate relationships do not influence which products we feature or how we describe them.
  • Transparency: We clearly mark external product links and affiliate relationships.
  • No False Claims: We do not invent reviews, ratings, prices, or sponsorships.
  • User First: We recommend products because we believe they offer value, not because of commission potential.

Marked Links

Product links on NeonVault are clearly labeled with "View Deal" or "Explore Product" language and may include affiliate disclosure badges. These indicate external merchant links.
